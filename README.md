### Greetings traveler 👋

I'm a PhD student at [KU Leuven](https://ror.org/05f950310), where I research Knowledge Graphs and AI. Previously, I was active as a Data Engineer at [VITO](https://ror.org/04gq0w522) (Flemish Institute for Technological Research).

I am currently working on:
- [REEVALUATE](https://reevaluate.eu/): Framework for safe, open, collaborative and inclusive digitization and management
of cultural heritage.

Where my focus is on:
- Ontologies for context in the Cultural Heritage domain.
- Neurosymbolic AI techniques for ontology alignment.
- AI techniques for contextualisation of digital artefacts.
- A search & retrieval system for reuse of digital artefacts.

I have contributed to other European research projects, such as:
- [PARC](https://www.eu-parc.eu/): Partnership for the Assessment of Risks from Chemicals.
- [INQUIRE](https://inquire-he.eu/): Improving indoor air quality for a healthier home and Europe.
- [EIRENE](https://www.eirene-ri.eu/): Environmental Exposure Assessment Research Infrastructure.

Where I was mostly part of work related to FAIR/linked data and data governance.
